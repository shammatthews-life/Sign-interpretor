# H47 Participant Research Framework (Deaf-Centred Co-Design)

- **Target Participants:** Adult fluent/native Deaf ISL signers (minimum pilot cohort `N = 6–10` across >= 2 Indian regions such as Delhi/NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, or Kolkata to capture regional variation).
- **Demographics Recorded (Pseudonymized):** ISL age of acquisition, primary daily communication modality, regional ISL background, familiarity with signing avatars, and comfort with English text.
- **Participatory Co-Design:** Participants are treated as linguistic experts and paid fair honoraria per session; Deaf interpreters/co-facilitators lead instructions so participants do not rely on written English.
