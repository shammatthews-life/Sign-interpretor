# H4 Sign Learn Access Supplement

This supplement applies to the WATER, I, ME, YOU, HELP, NEED, and DRINK intake records.

- **Official app sources**: Android `https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.islrtc`; iOS `https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sign-learn/id1644466408`.
- **Official status**: ISLRTC’s current homepage links both applications. Store metadata attributes Android to the Department of Empowerment of PwD / Indian Sign Language Research and Training Centre and iOS to Islrtc NewDelhi (© ISLRTC).
- **App access result**: the store listings were accessible; the app itself could not be installed or operated in this environment.
- **Candidate result**: for every candidate, exact-term search, entry label, variant labels, video loading, and video inspection are `NOT_INSPECTED`.
- **Authoring consequence**: no H4 candidate is authoring-eligible. This result does not assert that any term is absent from Sign Learn.

The manual capture protocol and full structured intake are in `docs/h4_signlearn_evidence_report.{md,json}`. Do not store source videos, screen recordings, or extracted frames in this repository without explicit permission; retain provenance and manually authored pose data only.
