## Visual Observations (Reference-Derived)

1. **Dominant Hand**:
   - RIGHT hand is dominant.
   - Left hand remains at rest/neutral.

2. **Handshape**:
   - Thumb and index finger tips are brought together, forming a circular contact loop ("O" / "F"-handshape).
   - Middle finger, ring finger, and little (pinky) finger remain open, extended upright, and slightly fanned.
   - The handshape is held stable throughout the gesture.

3. **Spatial Location**:
   - The right hand is raised to upper chest / shoulder / lower neck level on the right side of the body.
   - The hand is held in front of the torso/shoulder, NOT at the temple, NOT at the chin.

4. **Palm Orientation**:
   - Palm faces forward (towards the viewer / conversational partner).
   - Extended fingers point upwards.

5. **Wrist Orientation**:
   - Wrist is upright and neutral-to-slightly extended, maintaining forward presentation of the palm and circle.

6. **Movement Characteristics**:
   - Compact, controlled back-and-forth movement (anterior-posterior / sagittal pulse).
   - NOT a temple salute (unlike HELLO).
   - NOT an outward sweeping arc from the chin (rejecting old draft description).
   - NOT alphabet fingerspelling.
   - Hand returns cleanly to neutral resting pose after the movement.
