# H47 Ethics, Privacy & Informed Consent Framework

1. **Accessible ISL Video Consent:** Because written English is often a second language for Deaf signers, informed consent must be delivered via an ISL video explanation alongside plain-language summary cards, explicitly stating AI/avatar limitations and that the tool assists accessibility rather than replacing human ISL interpreters.
2. **Biometric & Video Privacy:** Any future participant video responses (e.g., signed meaning reconstructions) must be stored encrypted with pseudonymized participant IDs (`P01`, `P02`), never used for model training or facial-biometric harvesting without separate explicit opt-in consent, and deleted upon withdrawal.
3. **Voluntary Withdrawal & Fair Compensation:** Participants may skip any clip or withdraw at any time without penalty while keeping full session compensation.
