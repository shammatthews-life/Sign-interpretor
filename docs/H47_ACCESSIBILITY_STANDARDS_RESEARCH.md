# H47 Accessibility Standards Research

**Primary Standards Investigated:**
1. **ISO/IEC 20071-24** (*Information technology — User interface component accessibility — Part 24: Visual presentation of audio information in sign languages*; ITU-T twin `F.ACC-AVSL`).
2. **WCAG 2.2 Success Criterion 1.2.6** (*Sign Language (Prerecorded) — Level AAA*, W3C).
3. **ISO/IEC 20071-23** (*Visual presentation of audio information — captions/subtitles*, complementary layer).

---

## 1. Core Principles Across Standards

| Principle | ISO/IEC 20071-24 / ITU-T F.ACC-AVSL | WCAG 2.2 SC 1.2.6 (Level AAA) |
|---|---|---|
| **Linguistic Equivalence** | Sign languages are full natural languages with distinct grammar/syntax; translations must convey intended meaning, not literal word-for-word English | Rejects robotic Sign-Supported English; requires natural syntax, NMMs, and spatial grammar |
| **Visual Presentation & Framing** | Clear visibility of hands, fingers, face, and signing space; non-distracting high-contrast background | Signer/avatar framed hips/torso-up with clear facial & finger legibility; never an unreadable thumbnail |
| **Media Synchronization** | Tight synchronization between audio/video stream and signed presentation | Zero drift; playback controls (play, pause, seek, speed) tightly bound to main media |
| **User-Centred Evaluation** | Effectiveness must be evaluated for ease of understanding by target sign-language users | Automated tools cannot verify 1.2.6 compliance; evaluation by fluent native Deaf signers is mandatory |
| **Captions Are Complementary** | Written captions do not replace sign language for sign-dominant users | Captions + sign language are separate, complementary layers |
