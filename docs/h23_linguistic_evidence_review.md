## 1. Evidence Verification Lifecycle for High-Priority Signs

A rigorous audit was conducted across the top 100 highest-frequency signs in the library ([`data/h23_high_priority_sign_evidence.json`](../data/h23_high_priority_sign_evidence.json)). Each sign must advance through six sequential verification gates before being considered certified:
1. **Gate 1 — Dictionary Citation:** Verified presence in the official 10,000-word ISLRTC lexicon.
2. **Gate 2 — Video Demonstration Analysis:** Inspection of certified deaf master signer articulation videos.
3. **Gate 3 — 3D Motion Authoring:** Authoring keyframes conforming to Schema 2.0.0 with `{ x: 0, y: 0, z: 0 }` neutral transitions.
4. **Gate 4 — Technical Playability:** Successful real-time playback on Aether at 60 FPS in Three.js.
5. **Gate 5 — Biomechanical Inspection:** Verification of collision-free motion and natural joint rotations.
6. **Gate 6 — Independent Native Deaf Signer Approval:** Formal review by qualified ISL linguists.
