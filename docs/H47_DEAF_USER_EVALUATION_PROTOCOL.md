## 1. Protocol Design (No Linguistic Expertise Required)

- **Session Duration:** <= 35 minutes with breaks to prevent visual cognitive fatigue.
- **Blind Stimulus Presentation:** Captions and English transcripts are hidden during comprehension trials so participants evaluate the **avatar signing alone** rather than reading text.
- **Three Counterbalanced Blocks:**
  1. **Block A — Meaning Reconstruction & Distractor MC (`TRIAL-01`, `TRIAL-02`):** Free-form ISL/text meaning reconstruction followed by 4-option multiple choice containing 1 target + 3 controlled distractors (lexical, polarity-flip, role-reversal).
  2. **Block B — NMM & Spatial Minimal-Pair Discrimination (`TRIAL-03`, `TRIAL-04`):** Identical manual signs (`YOU HELP`) with varying NMMs or spatial loci to test whether non-manual/spatial cues actually convey grammatical distinctions to Deaf viewers.
  3. **Block C — Continuous Clip Readability & Speed Preference:** Likert ratings (1–5) for **Confidence**, **Naturalness**, and **Visual Readability** + open-ended feedback.
- **Guessing Control:** Separating confident comprehension from guessing via Confidence (1–5) weighting, polarity/role-reversal distractors, and open meaning reconstruction.
