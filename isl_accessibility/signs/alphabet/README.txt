PURPOSE & LINGUISTIC DISTINCTION:
This directory is reserved for Indian Sign Language (ISL) fingerspelling
alphabet reference visual assets (A through Z).

CRITICAL DISTINCTION:
ISL fingerspelling is strictly distinct from ISL lexical signs.
- Lexical Signs: Normal words in ISL (e.g. HELLO, WATER, GOOD) have their
  own distinct signs performed with hands, movement, and location.
- Fingerspelling: Used primarily for proper nouns (e.g. names of people,
  specific places) or rare words without an established lexical sign.
- FINGERSPELLING IS NOT A DIRECT REPLACEMENT FOR LEXICAL SIGNS.
  "HELLO" must not be signed as H -> E -> L -> L -> O.
  This directory exists for a planned fallback feature, kept strictly
  separate from the primary word-level translation library.

ASSET POLICY:
- Do NOT generate fake or AI-generated sign images.
- Do NOT scrape random Google Images or generic sign language sites.
- Do NOT use American Sign Language (ASL) fingerspelling charts (ISL uses
  distinct, largely two-handed fingerspelling forms).
- Only add images sourced and verified from legitimate ISL institutions,
  primarily the Indian Sign Language Research and Training Centre (ISLRTC).
